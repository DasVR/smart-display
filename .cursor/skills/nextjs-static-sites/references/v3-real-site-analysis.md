# Real Site Analysis — What to Steal for Portfolio v3

## Site 1: bymonolog.com

### Hero Area
- **Top**: NAV with "MONOLOG" logo left, "About · Work · Services · Process" center, "View All Projects" CTA right
- **Below nav**: Horizontal carousel/stat counter — "15+" with client description, navigation arrows
- **MASSIVE H1**: "MONOLOG" in giant bold sans-serif, spans almost full width, dark on light bg
- **Subheadline**: "Great founders changing the world deserve a presence as powerful as what they're building. Most founders we work with have built something significant, but their website doesn't show it yet. That gap costs more than revenue. It costs the certainty that your brand is finally being understood."
- **Founder attribution**: Circular headshot + "Huy (By Huy) Nguyen · Founder, MONOLOG"
- **Client logos**: Row of logos (Vinamilk, Sydney, OH, etc.) in grayscale

### "We close that gap" Section
- **Massive heading**: "We close ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ that gap" (with invisible-width characters creating gaps in text)
- **Body**: "Your website is where ideal customers decide if you're worth their time. We take what makes you irreplaceable, shape the entire experience around it, and make sure they feel that before they read another word."
- **Scattered image grid**: 5 images floating at different vertical positions, not aligned to a strict grid — organic, editorial feel

### Selected Work (Project Cards)
- **Format**: "SS /05" prefix on each (SS = selected stories, 05 = project count)
- **Card layout**: Left = project name + description, Right = massive metric number
- **OH Architecture**: "$2M+" — "In project enquiries generated within 3 months of launch"
- **Supersolid**: "58%" — "Increase in average session duration"
- **Mammoth Murals**: "$100K+" — "In new work within 30 days of launch"
- **HISS**: "15+" — "Global universities united on a single platform"
- **View All Stories**: "07 View All Stories (→)" — numbered CTA with arrow

### Process Section
- **Label**: "(REAL CLIENT STORIES)"
- **Heading**: "PROJECT JOURNEY"
- **Steps**:
  - "We uncover your story" — "We dig deep into your brand, surface what makes you irreplaceable, and shape it into sharp positioning and a website strategy."
  - "We shape your digital presence" — "With your narrative locked, we design and direct a brand and website that feels premium, signals credibility, and gives your audience one clear reason to lean in and act."
  - "We send it into the world" — "Your brand and website goes live as a long-term asset that turns attention into opportunity, attracts the clients you've built for, and grows with you."

### Services
- **Massive list**: "Brand Strategy · Visual Identity · Website Strategy · Website Design · Website Development · 3D Development"
- Displayed as giant text, one per line, stacked vertically

### CTA Section
- **Massive text**: "LET'S BUILD AN EXPERIENCE THAT MOVES PEOPLE"
- **Button**: "Tell us your story →"

### Footer
- **Left**: "MONOLOG" logo
- **Center**: Email, location (Hanoi City, Vietnam and Melbourne, Australia), social links
- **Right**: "Working Worldwide"

### What to Steal
1. **Massive typography** — let text be the design
2. **Stat counters with real metrics** — "$2M+", "58%", "$100K+"
3. **Scattered image grids** — not aligned, organic floating positions
4. **Invisible-width characters** in "We close that gap" for visual intrigue
5. **Numbered project prefix** — "SS /05" format
6. **Project cards with metric right column** — big number, small description
7. **Services as giant text list** — not bullet points, just stacked words
8. **Founder headshot + quote attribution** — humanizes the brand

## Typography Scale (from observations)

Monolog's heading sizes (approximate):
- H1 (MONOLOG): ~120-150px, bold, tight letter-spacing
- H2 (section heads): ~48-64px
- H3 (card titles): ~24-32px
- Body: ~16-18px, generous line-height
- Metrics: ~64-80px, bold

Mainframe's heading sizes:
- Similar massive scale
- Body text slightly smaller, more editorial
- Slash headers: ~14px for "/" and "(01)", ~48px for section name

## Animation Details to Implement

### On Load
- Stat numbers count up from 0 (duration: 1.5s, ease: expo.out)
- Hero text fades in with slight Y translate (duration: 0.8s, stagger: 0.1s)

### On Scroll
- Section headers: slash + number fade in first, then heading slides up
- Project cards: stagger from left, slight rotation (-2deg → 0deg)
- Images: parallax (move slower than scroll, ~0.8x speed)
- Services: each line slides in from left with 0.08s stagger

### On Hover
- Project cards: image scale 1.02, overlay darkens, metric number grows
- Service items: arrow slides in from left, text shifts right 8px
- Nav links: underline grows from center
- Buttons: text duplicates and slides (mainframe style)

### Cursor
- Custom small dot (8px, white, mix-blend-mode: difference)
- Expands to 40px ring on hover over links
- Slight lag follow using lerp or spring

### Grain/Texture
- CSS noise overlay: 3-5% opacity, mix-blend-mode: soft-light
- Gives that A24 / monolog cinematic feel
- Implementation: SVG feTurbulence filter or CSS noise texture

## Color Adaptation for Dark Theme

Since both reference sites are light-themed, adapt to our dark theme:

| Element | Light (Reference) | Dark (Ours) |
|---------|-------------------|-------------|
| Background | White / off-white | #0a0a0a |
| Text primary | Black | White |
| Text secondary | Gray-600 | Neutral-400 |
| Accents | Black | White or Orange |
| Borders | Light gray | Neutral-800 |
| Grain overlay | 3% black noise | 3% white noise |
