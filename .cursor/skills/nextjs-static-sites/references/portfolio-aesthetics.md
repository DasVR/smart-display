## Design Tokens for Arriq's Site
- Background: #0a0a0a (near-black)
- Text: #ffffff
- Muted: #a3a3a3 (neutral-400)
- Borders: #262626 (neutral-900)
- Accent: white (keep clean, or subtle warm tone)
- Display font: Bold + tight tracking
- Body font: Clean geometric sans
- Labels: Monospace, small caps, tracking-widest
